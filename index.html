Bounty rules 🎯▶

Global cap: 1 open entry per tier. If you have an open Bounty anywhere in your log, the Bounty option is hidden from the Instance dropdown until you resolve it.

Full price floors: Bounty ≥ $50, Big bounty ≥ $100, Mega bounty ≥ $250.

Date Logged is immutable on edit. Once saved, the readonly field can't be changed. Delete and recreate if needed.

Planned date: forward-only, unrestricted future. Any date today or later.

Greenlit gate: a checkbox between In Progress and Executed. Use it to mark "I've decided this will happen" without yet executing.

Roll-forward: open bounties display under the 1st of the current month in By date logged sort, and their recorded checkbox resets at month boundaries.

Month reset: on the first open of a new month, any open bounty whose Planned execution date now sits in a past month has that date (and time) cleared and a 🚩 appended to its description — the red card and blocked transcription checkbox force a review. Editing the entry requires setting a new planned date.

Date restrictions 📅▶

New non-planning entries: current calendar month, plus a 7-day grace back into the previous month at the start of each month.

New Planned expense planned date: today through unrestricted future.

New Bounty Date Logged: current month + 7-day grace. Planned date: today through unrestricted future.

Editing existing entries: picker relaxes to the first day of the month containing the entry's earliest existing date. Bounty Date Logged stays locked.

Retroactive warning fires for dates more than 7 days back (overridable).

Description conventions ✍️▶

Italics: wrap text in asterisks. *emphasized* renders italicized.

Person tagging: @name auto-capitalizes and colors blue. One token only (no spaces). Works in Description, Split with, and Recipient notes.

Recipient field: single-line input. For multiple recipients use @alex, @jordan or natural separators like @alex and @jordan.

Expense log — search & filters 🔎▶

Search box: a global query across each entry's description, location, category, instance and status labels, savings type/details, recipient note, split details, and amounts. Multiple words are AND-matched (all must appear).

Filters take priority: the Instance / Category / Status dropdowns decide what's on screen first; the search box only drills down within that subset. So filtering to "Food" and then searching narrows to Food entries that also match the query — search never widens past the filters.

Within-week order (By date logged): weeks stay chronological, but inside each week entries are grouped by priority — ☑️ executed planning entries first (by actual date), then ⏳ open planning entries (by planned date), then regular expenses (by date logged). Bounties count as planning entries. Priority outranks dragging: an entry that becomes executed always surfaces at the top of its week. Drag-and-drop reorders within a priority group (cross-group drags are blocked with a note), and a saved drag order is kept inside its group.

Reset filters clears the search box and all three dropdowns at once. It leaves the sort mode alone, since sort is a view preference rather than a filter.

Duplicate (⧉): opens a pre-filled Add expense draft from any card — copying description, full price, category, and location as a brand-new Expense. The instance, status, discount, split, recipient, tags, and dates are all reset (date defaults to today), and nothing is saved until you press Add, so you can tweak first or cancel. Handy for repeat purchases of the same thing.

Flagging incomplete entries 🚩▶

When you log something in a hurry and know it's missing details, drop a 🚩 in the description so you remember to come back and finish it.

  • Adding the flag: type /flag anywhere in the description and it becomes 🚩 inline (works while creating and while editing). You can also just type or paste the 🚩 emoji directly.
  • What it does: any entry whose description contains 🚩 turns light red in the Expense Log — an at-a-glance "fix me" signal.
  • Clearing it: a Reviewed? checkbox appears at the bottom-left of a flagged card. Ticking it removes the 🚩, restores the normal background, and the Reviewed control disappears. (You can also clear it by editing the entry and deleting the 🚩 yourself.)
  • Transcription gate: while an entry is flagged, its journal/Transcribe checkbox is disabled — you can't mark it transcribed until the flag is cleared. This keeps incomplete entries out of the planner.

Flagged entries never count as recorded, and the 🚩 is just text in the description — it has no effect on amounts, categories, or summaries.

Indulgence rules 🍬▶

5 slots per calendar month. Slots reset on the 1st. Slots numbered 1–5 sequentially in chronological order.

Weekly cap: 2 per Monday–Sunday week, except the final week of the month (no cap).

Pool keying (consumption-based): an indulgence belongs to the month it actually happens (or is scheduled to happen), not when it was committed:

  • Standalone indulgence: month of e.date
  • Planned + indulgence-tagged, Executed: month of actualDate
  • Planned + indulgence-tagged, any other status: month of plannedDate

Cap-aware edits: moving an indulgence into a month that already has 5 is blocked with a toast.

Dropdown behavior: only one slot offered at a time (the next sequential one). You can't skip ahead.

Journal page hints 📓▶

Everything is transcribed on the memo side of the Hobonichi Weeks spread. The checkbox tracks whether an entry has been transcribed yet.

Page hints:

  • Normal (non-planning) expenses: a checkbox labeled Transcribe to Memo. Tick it once transcribed.
  • ⏳ FRI 22 — open/unexecuted planning entry. The day-of-week + day number tell you which day's page the entry maps to.
  • ☑️ FRI 22 — executed planning entry. Same day-of-week + day number portion, hourglass swapped for a checkmark to signal it's done. Uses the actualDate.
  • ⏳ MON 1 — open bounties roll forward to the 1st of the current month for monthly check-ins.

When the checkbox appears:

  • Non-planning instances: always.
  • Open bounties: always (rolling forward to the 1st each month).
  • Other planning instances (including indulgence-tagged): when plannedDate or actualDate exists.

Checking the box dims the entry visually and persists across sessions. Open bounties reset their recorded state at month boundaries.

Flag gate: if an entry's description contains a 🚩, this checkbox is disabled until the flag is cleared (see Flagging incomplete entries).

Sticker placement workflow:

Inventory: 1 bounty sticker per tier per month (3 total) + 5 indulgence stickers per month. One sticker per tier/slot per month — that's it.

  • Bounty stickers: place the sticker on whichever day the page hint indicates at transcription time. If the bounty is still open, that's the 1st (⏳) — even if you transcribe mid- or late-month, the sticker goes on the 1st to mark "this commitment was on my radar this month." If the bounty has executed, the hint switches to ☑️ on the actualDate, and the sticker goes there. Either way: one sticker per tier per month.
  • Indulgence stickers: place on the day the page hint shows. While open, that's the planned date (⏳). Once executed, it switches to the actualDate (☑️). Use the slot-matching sticker.
  • Watch the symbol: ⏳ means "still open." ☑️ means "executed — transcribe the outcome." The symbol tells you which mode you're in without needing to look at the status badge.
Manage saved locations 📍▶

Every location you type is remembered and offered as a suggestion in the Location field. Fix a typo with rename, or remove ones you no longer want.

  • Rename (✎): if existing entries use the old name, you'll confirm a count — the new name is then written to those entries too, so the fix propagates everywhere.
  • Remove (×): deletes the suggestion. If entries still use it, you'll confirm and it's also cleared from those entries (otherwise it would reappear on the next save, since suggestions are re-harvested from your entries). A location used by no entries is removed without a prompt.
Planning fields 🗓️▶

Date logged: when you committed to the plan. For new entries: current month + 7-day grace into prior month. Locked on edit for bounties.

Status: In Progress / Executed / Canceled (+ Greenlit for bounties).

Planned date of execution: required. For Planned expense and Bounty, forward-only-unrestricted.

Actual date of execution: hidden until Status = Executed. Modal pops up to confirm. Defaults to planned date if planned date is today or earlier, else today. Cleared from storage if Status reverts.

Secondary tag (Planned expense only): None / Gift / Indulgence N.

Sign-in & sync 🔐▶

Sign-in window: once you sign in, you stay signed in on a rolling 72-hour basis. Every time you open the app the clock resets, so the sign-in screen only returns after roughly 72 hours of not opening it — not 72 hours after your last live session.

iOS caveat: the 72h timestamp lives in the same local storage iOS can purge for home-screen web apps. If iOS wipes storage entirely, the timestamp goes with it and you'll need to sign in again regardless — there's no way around that with browser storage alone.

Offline banner: if there's no live cloud session (or a save fails), an ⚠️ Offline banner appears under the tabs. Your edits still save on the device, but they aren't reaching the cloud until you sign in again. Sign out and back in to resume sync.

Savings shortcuts: /dsc → 🟠 Discover Cashback, /chs → 🔷 Chase Ultimate Reward, /ven → ✌🏻 Venmo Balance.

Inline flag: /flag expands to 🚩 anywhere in the description (not just as the whole field) to mark an entry as incomplete. See Flagging incomplete entries below.

Delta calculation in Savings Details — both directions trigger on a trailing space:

  • Forward $X.XX /delta + space → $X.XX Δ $Y.YY, where Y.YY = X.XX minus the amount paid (or full price if paid is 0/empty). Reads as "balance went from X to Y." Useful for the remaining gift card / store credit balance after an expense.
  • Backward /delta $Y.YY + space → $X.XX /delta $Y.YY, where X.XX = Y.YY plus the amount paid (fallback: full price). True inverse of forward: since forward subtracts paid to find the remaining balance, backward adds paid back to recover the original. Only fires when amount paid (or full price) is set.

Keyword autofill: typing certain keywords anywhere in the description sets Instance + Category. "gasoline" → Expense + Vehicle. "sonic fiber" → Subscription + Utilities. "fastrak" → Expense + Vehicle.

Summary tab 📊▶

Scope: every figure on the Summary tab is computed from your entire expense log across all months — it is not limited to the current month or to what's visible in the log view.

Stat boxes:

  • Total spent / Total saved / Full price value: realized figures. Planned expenses and bounties are excluded until they're executed, so these reflect money actually spent and saved.
  • Bounties total: amount paid across all executed bounty tiers (Bounty, Big bounty, Mega bounty).
  • Pending expenses / Pending savings: the projected amount and projected savings of planned expenses and bounties that haven't been executed yet. They move into the realized totals once you mark the entry Executed.

Split bills (net spend): Total spent and the by-instance / by-category breakdowns use net out-of-pocket for split entries. If others reimbursed you ("Others paid me"), the reimbursement is subtracted; if you only covered your share ("I paid others"), your share is used. Total saved and Full price value stay gross, since they track discounts rather than reimbursement.

By instance: a pie of realized spend by instance type, with Total spent in the center. Slices sum to Total spent (pending entries aren't included).

By category: the same realized spend, grouped by category.

Bounties & planned — by status: a count-based pie of every planned/bounty entry by status (Executed, In progress, Greenlit, Canceled), so you can see how many commitments have actually executed. The center shows the count of executed entries.

Charts redraw whenever you open the Summary tab.

Unsaved drafts 📝▶

If you start filling in the Add expense form and leave without pressing Add — reloading, switching apps, or closing the tab — your in-progress fields are kept and restored next time you open the app, with a brief "Restored unsaved draft" note.

This applies to new entries only, not edits of existing entries. The draft is saved continuously as you type (and flushed when the app is backgrounded, for iOS), and is cleared the moment you press Add or use the Clear button.
