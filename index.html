Indulgence rules▶

5 slots per calendar month. Slots reset on the 1st. Slots numbered 1–5 sequentially in chronological order.

Weekly cap: 2 per Monday–Sunday week, except the final week of the month (no cap).

Pool keying (consumption-based): an indulgence belongs to the month it actually happens (or is scheduled to happen), not when it was committed:

  • Standalone indulgence: month of e.date
  • Planned + indulgence-tagged, Executed: month of actualDate
  • Planned + indulgence-tagged, any other status: month of plannedDate

Cap-aware edits: moving an indulgence into a month that already has 5 is blocked with a toast.

Dropdown behavior: only one slot offered at a time (the next sequential one). You can't skip ahead.

Bounty rules▶

Global cap: 1 open entry per tier. If you have an open Bounty anywhere in your log, the Bounty option is hidden from the Instance dropdown until you resolve it.

Full price floors: Bounty ≥ $50, Big bounty ≥ $100, Mega bounty ≥ $250.

Date Logged is immutable on edit. Once saved, the readonly field can't be changed. Delete and recreate if needed.

Planned date: forward-only, unrestricted future. Any date today or later.

Greenlit gate: a checkbox between In Progress and Executed. Use it to mark "I've decided this will happen" without yet executing.

Roll-forward: open bounties display under the 1st of the current month in By date logged sort, and their recorded checkbox resets at month boundaries.

Planning fields▶

Date logged: when you committed to the plan. For new entries: current month + 7-day grace into prior month. Locked on edit for bounties.

Status: In Progress / Executed / Canceled (+ Greenlit for bounties).

Planned date of execution: required. For Planned expense and Bounty, forward-only-unrestricted.

Actual date of execution: hidden until Status = Executed. Modal pops up to confirm. Defaults to planned date if planned date is today or earlier, else today. Cleared from storage if Status reverts.

Secondary tag (Planned expense only): None / Gift / Indulgence N.

Savings shortcuts: /dsc → 🟠 Discover Cashback, /chs → 🔷 Chase Ultimate Reward, /ven → ✌🏻 Venmo Balance.

Delta calculation in Savings Details: type $X.XX /delta followed by a space. It replaces with $X.XX Δ $Y.YY where Y.YY is X.XX minus the amount paid (or full price if paid is 0/empty). Useful for tracking remaining gift card / store credit balance after an expense.

Keyword autofill: typing certain keywords anywhere in the description sets Instance + Category. "gasoline" → Expense + Vehicle. "sonic fiber" → Subscription + Utilities. "fastrak" → Expense + Vehicle.

Description conventions▶

Italics: wrap text in asterisks. *emphasized* renders italicized.

Person tagging: @name auto-capitalizes and colors blue. One token only (no spaces). Works in Description, Split with, and Recipient notes.

Recipient field: single-line input. For multiple recipients use @alex, @jordan or natural separators like @alex and @jordan.

Date restrictions▶

New non-planning entries: current calendar month, plus a 7-day grace back into the previous month at the start of each month.

New Planned expense planned date: today through unrestricted future.

New Bounty Date Logged: current month + 7-day grace. Planned date: today through unrestricted future.

Editing existing entries: picker relaxes to the first day of the month containing the entry's earliest existing date. Bounty Date Logged stays locked.

Retroactive warning fires for dates more than 7 days back (overridable).

Journal page hints▶

Everything is transcribed on the memo side of the Hobonichi Weeks spread. The checkbox tracks whether an entry has been transcribed yet.

Page hints:

  • Normal (non-planning) expenses: a checkbox labeled Transcribe to Memo. Tick it once transcribed.
  • ⏳ FRI 22 — open/unexecuted planning entry. The day-of-week + day number tell you which day's page the entry maps to.
  • ☑️ FRI 22 — executed planning entry. Same day-of-week + day number portion, hourglass swapped for a checkmark to signal it's done. Uses the actualDate.
  • ⏳ MON 1 — open bounties roll forward to the 1st of the current month for monthly check-ins.

When the checkbox appears:

  • Non-planning instances: always.
  • Open bounties: always (rolling forward to the 1st each month).
  • Other planning instances (including indulgence-tagged): when plannedDate or actualDate exists.

Checking the box dims the entry visually and persists across sessions. Open bounties reset their recorded state at month boundaries.

Sticker placement workflow:

Inventory: 1 bounty sticker per tier per month (3 total) + 5 indulgence stickers per month. One sticker per tier/slot per month — that's it.

  • Bounty stickers: place the sticker on whichever day the page hint indicates at transcription time. If the bounty is still open, that's the 1st (⏳) — even if you transcribe mid- or late-month, the sticker goes on the 1st to mark "this commitment was on my radar this month." If the bounty has executed, the hint switches to ☑️ on the actualDate, and the sticker goes there. Either way: one sticker per tier per month.
  • Indulgence stickers: place on the day the page hint shows. While open, that's the planned date (⏳). Once executed, it switches to the actualDate (☑️). Use the slot-matching sticker.
  • Watch the symbol: ⏳ means "still open." ☑️ means "executed — transcribe the outcome." The symbol tells you which mode you're in without needing to look at the status badge.
Summary tab▶

Scope: every figure on the Summary tab is computed from your entire expense log across all months — it is not limited to the current month or to what's visible in the log view.

Stat boxes:

  • Total spent / Total saved / Full price value: realized figures. Planned expenses and bounties are excluded until they're executed, so these reflect money actually spent and saved.
  • Bounties total: amount paid across all executed bounty tiers (Bounty, Big bounty, Mega bounty).
  • Pending expenses / Pending savings: the projected amount and projected savings of planned expenses and bounties that haven't been executed yet. They move into the realized totals once you mark the entry Executed.

By instance: a pie of realized spend by instance type, with Total spent in the center. Slices sum to Total spent (pending entries aren't included).

By category: the same realized spend, grouped by category.

Bounties & planned — by status: a count-based pie of every planned/bounty entry by status (Executed, In progress, Greenlit, Canceled), so you can see how many commitments have actually executed. The center shows the count of executed entries.

Charts redraw whenever you open the Summary tab.
